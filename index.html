Apple Social Media Analytics

An Excel project on how Apple's posts, hashtags, campaigns and followers perform across four platforms.

About this project

I analysed Apple's social media data on Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter and YouTube in Excel, using pivot tables and charts. The project has six parts: cleaning the data, engagement, platforms, hashtags and content, campaigns, and follower growth.

300 posts analysed
4 platforms
5 campaigns
12.3% average engagement rate

Words used in this project

Engagement rate
Likes + shares + comments, divided by impressions. It shows how many views turned into action.
Impressions
The number of times a post was shown on a screen.
Reach
The number of different people who saw a post.
Engagement uplift
How much a campaign increased engagement, shown as a percentage.
Moving average
An average of the last few weeks. It smooths out sudden ups and downs so the trend is easier to see.

The six parts of the project

Part 1

Cleaning the data

The cleaned dataset has 300 posts from June 2024 to October 2025. Each post has a platform, content type, hashtags, likes, shares, comments, clicks, reach, impressions and campaign name. The hashtags were split into two columns, Hashtag_1 and Hashtag_2. There are no duplicate posts and no missing values, except Hashtag_2 for the 33 posts that use only one hashtag.

  • The Apple Event September 2025 campaign had the highest engagement uplift (47%).
  • The iPhone 16 Launch had the most impressions (950,000), but it also cost the most and gave the fewest impressions for each unit spent.
  • The Sustainability Awareness Drive cost the least and gave the most impressions for each unit spent.
  • Across all five campaigns, total ad spend was 315,000, total impressions were 3.86 million, and the average uplift was 40.6%.
  • Campaigns with high engagement performed better than campaigns that only had a high budget.

Part 6

Follower retention and loyalty

Smoothed follower growth (3-week moving average)

  • The smoothed trend rises steadily, from about 0.57M on 2024-06-17 to about 4.64M on 2025-05-12.
  • The moving average makes the trend easier to read by reducing sudden weekly jumps.
  • I also compared ad spend with weekly follower growth. In this data, higher spending did not clearly lead to more new followers.

What Apple could do next

  • Post more video and carousel content, and use images to support them.
  • Use hashtags that work: #iPhone16Launch #AppleEvent for likes and clicks, and #AppleWatchUltra2 #HealthTech for shares.
  • Work on LinkedIn engagement, which is the lowest of the four platforms.
  • Grow YouTube followers, since YouTube engagement is already high.
  • Plan campaigns around engagement, not just a bigger budget.
